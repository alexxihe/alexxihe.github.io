# Discussion slides

Upload discussion slides (PDF) to this folder. They appear automatically in
the Discussions section of the website, newest first.

Name each file like this:

    YYYY-MM Conference - Paper Title - Authors.pdf

For example:

    2025-01 AFA - The Labor Market Effects of Mergers - Smith and Jones.pdf
    2024-05 Duke-UNC Asset Pricing Conference - Paper Title - Lee.pdf

- `YYYY-MM` is the year and month of the conference (sorting uses it).
- The parts are separated by " - " (space, hyphen, space), so avoid that
  sequence inside the conference name or title.
- The authors part is optional.
