Alex Xi He

Assistant Professor of Finance
Robert H. Smith School of Business
University of Maryland

My research studies corporate finance, labor and finance, innovation and entrepreneurship, and organizational economics. Recent work examines how firms' investments in artificial intelligence affect growth, productivity, and risk; how mergers and labor market power shape innovation; and how managers and household finances affect workers' wages and careers.

I received my PhD in Economics from MIT in 2019 and my bachelor's degree in Economics and Mathematics from Tsinghua University.
